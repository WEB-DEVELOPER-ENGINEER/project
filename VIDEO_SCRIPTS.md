## 🎯 Voiceover Direction Notes

### **Tone Guidelines**:
- **Overall Feel**: Professional but approachable, like a trusted business advisor
- **Pace**: Moderate speed (140-160 words per minute)
- **Energy**: Building from concern to excitement to confidence
- **Accent**: Neutral, internationally understood
- **Gender**: Test both male and female options

### **Emotional Journey**:
1. **Empathy** (Problems): "We understand your frustration"
2. **Hope** (Solution): "There's a better way"
3. **Excitement** (Features): "Look what you can do"
4. **Confidence** (Results): "This really works"
5. **Urgency** (CTA): "Don't wait, start now"

### **Key Emphasis Words**:
- **"YOU"** (empowerment)
- **"Control"** (independence)
- **"Professional"** (quality)
- **"Lightning-fast"** (performance)
- **"Everything"** (completeness)

## 🎨 Visual Style Guide

### **Color Palette**:
- **Primary Orange**: #e86e2a (CTAs, highlights)
- **Primary Blue**: #0a447c (headers, trust elements)
- **Success Green**: #22c55e (performance metrics)
- **Warning Red**: #ef4444 (problem identification)
- **Neutral Gray**: #64748b (supporting text)

### **Typography**:
- **Headlines**: Bold, sans-serif (matching website)
- **Body Text**: Clean, readable sans-serif
- **Metrics**: Monospace for technical data
- **CTAs**: Bold, action-oriented

### **Animation Principles**:
- **Smooth Transitions**: 300-500ms easing
- **Purposeful Motion**: Every animation serves a purpose
- **Performance Focus**: 60fps, optimized rendering
- **Brand Consistency**: Matches website animations

## 🎵 Music & Audio Guidelines

### **Audio Levels**:
- **Voiceover**: -12dB to -6dB (primary focus)
- **Music**: -24dB to -18dB (supporting)
- **Sound Effects**: -18dB to -12dB (accent only)

### **Sync Points**:
- **Problem Section**: Minor key, building tension
- **Solution Reveal**: Key change to major, volume increase
- **Feature Demo**: Rhythmic, productive feeling
- **CTA**: Strong, memorable finish
